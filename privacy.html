Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

CoinsRich ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CoinsRich mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The CoinsRich app:

  • Does not require you to create an account
  • Does not collect your name, email, or phone number
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, or files
  • Does not use analytics or advertising SDKs

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Price alerts — the target prices you set for specific coins are saved on your device using encrypted local storage. This data never leaves your device.
  • App preferences — your selected sort mode and search history are stored locally.

You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App.

3. Network Requests

When you use the App, it makes requests to the CoinsRich API (api.coinsrich.com) to fetch live cryptocurrency prices. These requests:

  • Do not include any personal identifiers
  • Are not logged with your IP address
  • Only transmit price data and public market information

4. Third-Party Services

Cryptocurrency price data is provided by Binance's public API. We encourage you to review Binance's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data.

5. Children's Privacy

CoinsRich does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The App does not require registration and does not collect any user data.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
